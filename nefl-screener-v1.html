MercadoLibre
NASDAQ: MELI
Founder-Chair tier — transitioned Jan 2026
HQ: Argentina/Uruguay · Founder: Marcos Galperin — moved from CEO to Executive Chairman on Jan 1, 2026 · Network effect: Latin America's largest marketplace + Mercado Pago fintech ecosystem
This is exactly the case the tiered rule exists for: Galperin founded the company in 1999 and ran it as CEO for 26 years, but stepped back to Executive Chairman starting this January. Under the classification rule, that puts MercadoLibre in the Founder-Chair tier — recently transitioned, still holds a board leadership role, retains a significant ownership stake — rather than being silently dropped (strict CEO-only rule) or silently treated the same as an active founder-CEO (loose rule). You see the tier and decide how much weight to give it.
